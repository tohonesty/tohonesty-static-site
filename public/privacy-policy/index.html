Tohonesty – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tohonesty Limited collects and uses personal information when you visit tohonesty.com, submit an enquiry or communicate with us. Please read it before providing personal information.

1. Who we are

Tohonesty Limited is the controller of the personal information described in this policy. This means that we determine why and how that information is processed.

  • Company: Tohonesty Limited
  • Company number: 15547832
  • ICO registration: ZB778271
  • Registered office: The Old School Rooms, The Street, North Stoke, Wallingford, United Kingdom, OX10 6BL
  • Privacy contact: security@tohonesty.com

Tohonesty is an independent introduction and referral service. We are not a law firm, recovery contractor, financial adviser, forensic laboratory or asset custodian.

2. Scope

This policy applies to personal information processed by Tohonesty in connection with this website, our initial enquiry process, subsequent communications and authorised introductions. It does not govern an independent specialist's processing. A specialist will provide its own privacy information if you communicate with or engage it.

3. Information you provide through the contact form

Our contact form is deliberately limited. It collects:

  • your full name;
  • your email address;
  • your selected ownership or evidentiary-status response; and
  • confirmation that you have read the relevant notice and consent to the submission and contact requested by the form.

Submitting an enquiry is voluntary. We require the form fields so that we can identify the sender, reply and make a preliminary assessment against our referral criteria. If you do not provide them, we cannot receive or respond to the enquiry through the form.

Do not provide a case narrative, evidence or sensitive technical information. We never request or accept private keys, seed phrases, passwords, PINs, passphrases, recovery codes or direct access to a wallet, account, device or asset.

4. Evidence and allegations must not be sent to Tohonesty

Tohonesty does not request or accept case evidence, allegations concerning identifiable persons, wallet addresses, transaction records, court documents or detailed loss information.

If such material is sent to us unsolicited, we will not assess it, use it to decide the legal merits of a matter, add it to an enquiry record or forward it to a specialist. We will delete it from our active systems as soon as reasonably practicable and normally within seven days, unless temporary retention is required by law or necessary to protect the security of our systems.

We may retain a minimal deletion record containing the date received, source address, date deleted and reason for deletion for up to 12 months. The deletion record will not reproduce or summarise the evidence or allegation. Substantive material should be provided only through a secure process established by an independently engaged specialist.

5. How the secure contact form works

The contact form encrypts its contents in your browser before the network submission occurs. The public contact endpoint receives an encrypted envelope rather than the plaintext form contents. That envelope is transmitted to Tohonesty through our communications infrastructure and is reviewed using a controlled offline decryption process.

Authorised decryption is necessary for us to read your name, email address, selected response and consent confirmation and to reply to you. The encrypted envelope and associated delivery records remain personal information where they relate to you or can be decrypted by us.

This browser encryption applies to the initial contact form. It does not automatically apply to later ordinary email, telephone or other communications. No system can eliminate every security risk, and this policy does not promise absolute confidentiality or security.

6. Information collected when you use the website

When you visit the website, our hosting and security provider may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the service. Depending on the request, this may include:

  • IP address and approximate network or geographic information derived from it;
  • date, time, requested address, request method and response status;
  • browser, device, protocol and request-header information;
  • security signals, firewall results, rate-limit events, bot or challenge results; and
  • performance, diagnostic and error information.

Technical records may identify or distinguish a device or visitor and are not described as anonymous merely because they do not contain a name.

We do not use advertising cookies, behavioural advertising, third-party marketing analytics or cross-site tracking on this website.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

Cloudflare may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary to deliver and secure the website, manage traffic, apply rate limits or record that a browser has passed a security challenge. Their names and duration may vary according to the security function in use.

We rely on the applicable communication or strictly necessary exemption only where the technology is essential to provide or protect the service requested by the visitor. These technologies are not used by Tohonesty to create advertising profiles. Because we currently use only exempt security and operational technologies, we do not display a consent banner. We will review this position before introducing any non-essential technology.

You can remove or block cookies using your browser settings, although doing so may cause a security challenge to repeat or prevent part of the website from functioning correctly.

8. Information collected during later communications

If we reply to an enquiry, we may process ordinary contact and correspondence information such as your email address, telephone number, organisation, role, availability and communications with us.

We will keep those communications limited to determining whether an introduction may be appropriate and administering an authorised introduction. We do not use later communications to collect evidence, investigate allegations, provide legal advice, undertake forensic analysis or manage a recovery matter.

9. Where information comes from

We obtain contact information directly from you. Technical and security information is generated when your device communicates with our website and may be provided to us by Cloudflare.

We do not purchase marketing lists, scrape personal contact details from public registers or use background marketing trackers to obtain information about prospective clients.

10. Why we use personal information

We may use personal information to:

  • receive, decrypt and respond to an enquiry;
  • conduct a preliminary suitability review against our value, geographic and service criteria;
  • confirm identity and communication details where reasonably necessary;
  • seek your permission for a proposed introduction;
  • make and document an authorised introduction;
  • administer our relationship with an independent specialist, including an introduction fee where applicable;
  • operate, secure, test and defend the website and contact service;
  • detect and respond to misuse, fraud, impersonation and security incidents;
  • respond to privacy requests and complaints; and
  • comply with legal, regulatory, accounting and tax obligations and establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Our preliminary review is not a legal opinion, forensic assessment or determination of ownership, legal merits, technical recoverability or prospects of recovery.

11. Lawful bases

Depending on the processing, we rely on:

  • Legitimate interests: receiving and responding to genuine enquiries; deciding whether our limited referral service may be appropriate; operating a responsible introduction business; documenting authorised introductions; securing our systems; preventing misuse and impersonation; and protecting our legal and commercial interests. We consider the limited nature of the information, the expectations of a person who contacts us and the safeguards described in this policy.
  • Consent: where we ask for your specific permission before disclosing your contact information to an identified independent specialist, or where consent is otherwise the appropriate basis. You may withdraw consent before the relevant processing by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect processing already undertaken lawfully and does not require deletion where another lawful basis or legal obligation applies.
  • Legal obligation: where processing is necessary to meet an obligation imposed on us, including applicable company, accounting, tax and data-protection duties.

The contact-form checkbox records your acknowledgement of the notice and your request to submit the form and be contacted. It does not remove your statutory rights or convert every later use of information into consent-based processing.

12. No automated decisions or marketing

We do not make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about prospective clients solely by automated processing. A person reviews a successfully decrypted enquiry.

We do not use enquiry information for unrelated direct marketing and do not sell it to data brokers or advertising networks.

13. Who receives personal information

Where necessary for the purposes described above, personal information may be processed by or disclosed to:

  • Cloudflare: our website hosting, content-delivery, network-security and encrypted-notification transmission provider. Cloudflare processes website and security metadata and the encrypted contact envelope on our behalf.
  • Google Workspace: our business email and communications provider. The initial contact notification is delivered in encrypted form, while later ordinary email communications may contain plaintext information supplied in those later communications.
  • Professional and administrative advisers: accountants, auditors, insurers, legal advisers and other providers where access is necessary for their defined function and subject to confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • An independent specialist: only after we identify the proposed recipient and obtain your express permission to disclose the relevant contact information. The specialist then acts as an independent controller and is responsible for its own processing.
  • Public authorities, regulators or courts: where disclosure is required by law or is necessary and proportionate for legal claims, regulatory compliance or the prevention or detection of unlawful activity.

Our processors may use approved subprocessors under their contractual arrangements with us. We do not give infrastructure providers private keys or recovery credentials because we do not collect those credentials.

14. International transfers

Cloudflare, Google and their approved subprocessors may process information outside the United Kingdom. Where this involves a restricted transfer, we require it to be covered by applicable UK adequacy regulations or another lawful safeguard, such as an approved international data transfer agreement or UK addendum, together with any required data-protection assessment.

Tohonesty will only make an authorised introduction to an independent specialist outside the United Kingdom where the proposed recipient is covered by applicable UK adequacy regulations. Before making the introduction, we check the status of the relevant country, territory, sector or organisation and obtain your express permission to disclose your contact information.

You may contact us for further information about the transfer mechanism relevant to your information.

15. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information in a manner appropriate to the risk. These include data minimisation, browser-side encryption for the initial form, restricted access, controlled offline decryption, account security controls and protections supplied by our infrastructure providers.

Only an authorised person may access decrypted contact submissions on a need-to-know basis. We do not maintain the public website as a case-file or evidence repository.

If you suspect misuse of your information or impersonation of Tohonesty, contact security@tohonesty.com.

16. Personal data breaches

We maintain procedures for identifying, containing, assessing and documenting suspected personal data breaches. Where a breach is likely to create a risk to individuals' rights and freedoms, we will notify the Information Commissioner's Office without undue delay and, where feasible, within 72 hours after becoming aware of it. Where the breach is likely to create a high risk, we will also notify affected individuals without undue delay, unless an applicable exception permits otherwise.

17. Retention

We apply the following standard periods. We may delete information earlier where it is no longer needed. We may retain particular information for longer where required by law, where a rights request or complaint is unresolved, or where it is necessary for an actual or reasonably anticipated legal or regulatory matter.

  • Website and security records available to Tohonesty: normally up to 30 days, unless connected with a specific security incident.
  • Failed, blocked or incomplete form requests: we do not deliberately retain form contents; related security metadata follows the period above.
  • Unsuitable, declined or abandoned enquiry: six months after our decision or last substantive contact.
  • Request not to proceed before referral: deletion from active systems within 30 days, except for a minimal record needed to document the request or meet another lawful requirement.
  • Unsolicited evidence or allegations: normally deleted from active systems within seven days; the minimal deletion record described in section 4 may be retained for 12 months.
  • Ordinary correspondence following a successful introduction: 12 months after the introduction, unless it forms part of a record retained for another stated purpose.
  • Referral permission and essential referral record: six years from the end of the financial year in which the introduction occurred or the final associated fee was received, whichever is later.
  • Commission, invoice and accounting records: six years from the end of the company financial year to which they relate, or longer where required by law.
  • Privacy-rights requests and complaints: three years after closure.

The encrypted notification is retained according to the category and purpose of the corresponding enquiry; encryption is not a reason to keep it indefinitely. At the end of a period, information is deleted from active systems or anonymised. Residual copies may remain temporarily in restricted backups until overwritten under the provider's normal cycle. Backup copies are not returned to ordinary use except where necessary for disaster recovery.

18. Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may have the right to:

  • request access to your personal information;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • ask us to erase information where the right to erasure applies;
  • ask us to restrict processing in applicable circumstances;
  • receive certain information in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format where the right to data portability applies;
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on consent; and
  • complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Your right to object: You have the right to object to processing based on our legitimate interests. If you object, we will stop the relevant processing unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing is needed for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

These rights are subject to statutory conditions, restrictions and exemptions and do not all apply in every situation. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify your identity. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

To exercise a right, contact security@tohonesty.com.

19. Complaints

You may make a data-protection complaint by emailing security@tohonesty.com. Please explain what you believe has happened, identify the personal information or processing involved and tell us what you would like us to do. You may complain on behalf of another person where you have appropriate authority to do so.

We will acknowledge a data-protection complaint within 30 days of receipt. Without undue delay, we will take appropriate steps to investigate it, make any enquiries reasonably necessary, keep you informed of progress where appropriate and tell you the outcome. We may ask for information needed to confirm your identity or authority, but will not request more than is reasonably necessary.

You may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. You do not have to surrender that right by contacting us first:

ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/

20. External websites

This website may link to websites operated by third parties. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices. We are not responsible for a third party's website merely because we provide a link to it.

21. Children

Our service is intended for adults acting personally or for organisations and is not directed to children. A person under 18 should not submit an enquiry through the contact form.

22. Business changes

If Tohonesty undergoes a genuine sale, merger, restructuring or transfer, relevant personal information may be disclosed under appropriate confidentiality restrictions or transferred where there is a lawful basis and the transfer is necessary for that transaction. We will provide any additional privacy information required by law. Statutory data-protection rights are not removed by a business transfer.

23. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in law, our providers or our processing. The current version and its revision date will be published on this page. Where a change materially affects existing processing, we will take reasonable steps to bring it to the attention of affected individuals where required. Continued website use is not treated as consent to processing that legally requires consent.
