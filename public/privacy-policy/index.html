Tohonesty – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 05 August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies between you, the User of this Website, and Tohonesty, the owner and provider of this Website. Tohonesty takes the privacy of your information very seriously. This Privacy Policy applies to our use of any and all Data collected by us or provided by you in relation to your use of the Website.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully.

Definitions and Interpretation

1. In this Privacy Policy, the following definitions are used:

  • Data: collectively all information that you submit to Tohonesty via the Website. This definition incorporates, where applicable, the definitions provided in the Data Protection Laws;
  • Data Protection Laws: any applicable law relating to the processing of personal Data, including but not limited to the GDPR, and any national implementing and supplementary laws, regulations and secondary legislation;
  • GDPR: the UK General Data Protection Regulation;
  • Tohonesty, we or us: Tohonesty of The Old School Rooms, The Street, North Stoke, Oxfordshire OX10 6BL;
  • User or you: any third party that accesses the Website and is not either (i) employed by Tohonesty and acting in the course of their employment or (ii) engaged as a consultant or otherwise providing services to Tohonesty and accessing the Website in connection with the provision of such services; and
  • Website: the website that you are currently using, tohonesty.com, and any sub-domains of this site unless expressly excluded by their own terms and conditions.

2. In this Privacy Policy, unless the context requires a different interpretation:

  • the singular includes the plural and vice versa;
  • references to sub-clauses, clauses, schedules or appendices are to sub-clauses, clauses, schedules or appendices of this Privacy Policy;
  • a reference to a person includes firms, companies, government entities, trusts and partnerships;
  • “including” is understood to mean “including without limitation”;
  • reference to any statutory provision includes any modification or amendment of it;
  • the headings and sub-headings do not form part of this Privacy Policy.

Scope of this Privacy Policy

3. This Privacy Policy applies only to the actions of Tohonesty and Users with respect to this Website. It does not extend to any websites that can be accessed from this Website including, but not limited to, any links we may provide to social media websites.

4. For purposes of the applicable Data Protection Laws, Tohonesty is the “data controller”. This means that Tohonesty determines the purposes for which, and the manner in which, your Data is processed.

Data Collected

5. When you submit a query through our online contact portal, we collect only the minimum information needed to decide whether an introduction may be appropriate. This information is provided voluntarily by you and is limited to:

  • Your full name and email address.
  • Your ownership status — whether you currently possess (or believe you can obtain) technical or factual evidence that links you to the digital assets in question.

We deliberately do not collect asset type, valuation figures, blockchain network, wallet addresses, transaction details, private keys, seed phrases or any other technical recovery information at this stage.

Important notes:

  • Our service is only intended for claims that meet the minimum threshold explained in our FAQs. Submitting a request implies that your claim meets that threshold.
  • Ownership status is the single most important factor in whether recovery is realistically possible. Many people either already hold usable evidence of ownership without realising it, or need specialist help to obtain it. We will discuss this with you only if we decide a referral may be suitable.
  • Providing private keys, seed phrases, passwords, PINs or recovery codes to anyone (including us) is extremely dangerous and is never requested or accepted by Tohonesty.

How We Collect Data

6. Data That is Given to Us by You: Tohonesty collects personal Data directly from you when you actively engage with our platform. To protect consumer privacy and enforce strict Data security, we utilise a single-channel preliminary intake protocol:

  • Mandatory Form Intake: Case records are opened exclusively using information submitted directly through our secure online Case Intake Form. In line with our strict Data minimisation policy, we do not accept or process initial asset recovery intakes via free-text emails, telephone conversations, or physical post. Any unsolicited intake Data sent through these excluded communication methods will be securely deleted.
  • Subsequent Communications: Once a secure Case Intake Form has been officially submitted and evaluated for minimum viability, we will be in contact with you on how to proceed via the email you used in the form. We may utilise telephone, direct email, or other official communication channels to discuss your verified case details further.

7. Data That is Scraped: We do not scrape Data from external public registers, purchase marketing leads, or deploy background tracking scripts to capture your personal contact details.

8. Data That is Collected Automatically: To the extent that you access the Website, we will collect your Data automatically, for example:

  • Automated Server Performance: To maintain site reliability, protect against digital attacks, and monitor page loading speeds, our network infrastructure providers process anonymous, high-level server Data. This includes technical information like browser types and server response times. This Data is entirely aggregated, does not identify individual visitors, and is used strictly for server health and network optimisation.
  • Cookies and Security Technologies: Our Website strictly uses necessary session and security cookies to maintain site stability, block malicious traffic, and remember basic User preferences. In accordance with the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), these technical assets (such as CDN bot-protection tokens) are classified as strictly necessary for core operations and do not track personal identities or build User advertising profiles. We do not run third-party marketing analytics or cross-site tracking scripts.
  • Managing Your Preferences: Because our Website strictly limits cookie deployment to these essential security and functional variables, an interactive tracking opt-out cookie banner is not required. However, you retain full control over your local device settings and can delete or block these essential cookies at any time by clearing your browser cache or modifying your browser’s privacy settings. We do not share any automated platform Data with advertising networks or cross-site tracking profiles.

How We Use Your Data

9. We process your personal Data strictly to provide you with a secure, efficient experience on our Website and to evaluate your inquiry. Specifically, your Data is used to:

  • Assess the validity of your digital asset claim and determine the likelihood of a successful recovery.
  • Evaluate whether your case meets the structural criteria for referral to our specialist Data recovery partners.

Following a successful referral, our use of your Data is strictly limited to internal administrative tracking and managing associated commission or referral fees. Because our service is limited exclusively to making introductions, we do not provide ongoing legal advice, case management, or technical updates once the referral is complete.

10. Lawful Basis for Processing: Under the GDPR, we rely on the following legal grounds to process your personal Data:

  • Legitimate Interests: We process your Data to assess and manage digital asset recovery leads, improve our platform’s utility, and determine if your case is a strong match for our specialist referral network. If you object to this processing, you have the right to challenge it under certain conditions (please see the “Your Rights” section below).
  • Consent: We will always obtain your explicit, unambiguous consent before sharing any of your personal information with our recovery partners.

You have the absolute right to withdraw your consent at any time. To do so, please contact our Data security team directly at security@tohonesty.com.

Who We Share Data With

11. With your prior agreement, we may share your Data with the following groups of people for the following reasons:

  • Our Specialist Recovery Partners: As a client referral service, Tohonesty may refer suitable claimed cases to a small number of specialist asset recovery firms. Some of these partners are based in different countries. We only use partners officially recognised by the UK Government as providing an adequate level of Data protection, ensuring your Data remains secure.
  • Independent Responsibility: We will always contact you to obtain your explicit permission before sharing any of your Data with a recovery partner. Once shared, the recovery partner becomes an independent data controller and will process your Data under their own privacy policy. We do not provide joint case management or ongoing oversight following this introduction.

Keeping Your Data Secure

12. We implement robust technical and organisational security measures designed to safeguard your personal Data against unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. These safeguards include:

  • Restricted Access: Access to your information is strictly limited to authorised personnel and controlled via secure cloud environments utilising multi-factor authentication (MFA) and passkeys.
  • Secure Storage: All Data is hosted on encrypted, high-security cloud servers with continuous monitoring.

13. Data Breach Procedures: We maintain clear, internal protocols to identify and respond swiftly to any suspected personal Data breach. If a breach occurs that poses a risk to your rights, we will notify the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) and all affected individuals without undue delay, fully complying with the strict timelines set by the GDPR.

If you suspect any loss, misuse, or unauthorised access to your Data, please report it immediately to our security response team at security@tohonesty.com.

Data Retention Periods

14. Unless an extended retention period is required or permitted by applicable law, we will only retain your personal Data on our systems for the duration necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy. Specifically:

  • Unreferred Lead Data: Case inquiries that do not proceed to a referral will be held for a maximum of six (6) months from the date of our last contact, after which they will be permanently deleted.
  • Referred Cases: Data for successfully referred cases will be retained until our specialist recovery partner confirms the case is closed. We will permanently delete this Data within thirty (30) days of receiving that closure confirmation.

You may formally request the erasure of your Data at any time by contacting us at security@tohonesty.com.

15. Backup and Archival Storage: Please note that even following a formal deletion request or the expiration of the retention periods above, certain Data may temporarily persist on encrypted backup or archival media. This Data is isolated from active processing and is maintained strictly for compliance with legal, tax, or statutory regulatory obligations for a limited period.

Your Legal Rights

16. Under the GDPR, you possess specific statutory rights regarding how your personal information is managed. These rights include:

  • Right to Access: You have the right to request copies of the personal Data we hold about you at any time. We will not charge you for this service unless your request is manifestly unfounded or excessive. If we are legally permitted to refuse your request, we will provide you with a clear explanation of our decision.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal Data.
  • Right to Erasure: Also known as the “right to be forgotten,” this allows you to request the permanent removal of your Data from our active systems.
  • Right to Restriction: You have the right to restrict or “block” the processing of your Data, limiting how we are permitted to use it.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request that we transfer, move, or copy your Data to another service provider in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You have the absolute right to object to our processing of your Data, including where we rely on legitimate interests as our lawful basis.

17. Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these statutory rights, ask questions about this Privacy Policy, or formally withdraw your consent, please submit a request to our Data protection team at security@tohonesty.com.

18. How to Raise a Complaint

If you are dissatisfied with how we handle your personal Data, we encourage you to contact us directly so we can resolve the issue. However, you maintain the right to lodge a formal complaint with the relevant supervisory authority. In the United Kingdom, this is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). You can find their current contact channels and submission forms directly on their official website at https://ico.org.uk.

19. Keeping Your Information Updated

We must maintain accurate and up-to-date records. Please notify us immediately if any of your personal details change at any point during your engagement with us.

Links to Other Websites

20. Our Website may contain hyperlinks to external websites operated by third parties, including our specialist asset recovery partners. We possess no operational control over these external platforms and assume no responsibility or liability for their content, security, or Data handling protocols. This Privacy Policy applies strictly to this Website; therefore, we strongly advise you to review the privacy statements and terms of use of any third-party websites before providing them with your personal Data.

Business Transfers and Control

21. Business Transfers and Control

From time to time, we may expand, reduce, or restructure our operations, which may involve the sale, merger, or transfer of control of all or part of Tohonesty. If such a transaction occurs, User Data relevant to the transferred portion of the business will only be shared or transferred after we have obtained your explicit permission. Following the transfer, the new owner or controlling party will be bound by the terms of this Privacy Policy and permitted to use your Data strictly for the original purposes for which it was collected.

22. Prospective Buyers

We may also disclose anonymised or necessary User Data to potential buyers evaluating our business operations or assets, only after getting your consent. No personally identifiable Data will ever be shared with prospective purchasers during due diligence without your prior consent.

23. Commitment to Privacy Protection

In all scenarios involving a business transition, corporate sale, or asset transfer, we will implement strict technical and contractual safeguards to ensure your Data security and privacy rights remain fully protected.
