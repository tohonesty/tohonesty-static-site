Before you begin

The form contents are encrypted in this browser before the network submission occurs. The public endpoint and email body receive an encrypted envelope rather than the plaintext form contents.

  • Do not provide private keys or seed phrases.
  • Do not provide passwords, PINs, passphrases or recovery codes.
  • Do not provide allegations, case evidence, wallet addresses, transaction details or asset values at this stage.

There is no free-text field by design. Submitting this form requests only a preliminary review by Tohonesty. It does not engage a specialist or authorise an introduction.
