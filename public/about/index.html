About Tohonesty

A discreet introduction service connecting qualifying digital-asset owners and institutions with independent legal and forensic specialists.

What we do

Tohonesty is a UK-based introduction and referral service with an international outlook. We consider enquiries involving high-value digital assets and, where an introduction appears appropriate, connect the prospective client with independent specialists able to assess the relevant legal, jurisdictional, evidential and technical issues.

Our role is deliberately limited. Tohonesty does not provide legal, financial, investment, tracing, forensic or recovery services. We do not take custody of assets, operate wallets or hold client credentials. Any specialist decides independently whether to accept a matter and provides its own advice, terms, assessment and services directly to the client.

Digital assets and property rights

The law of England and Wales is capable of recognising certain digital assets as objects of personal property rights. The Property (Digital Assets etc) Act 2025 confirms that a thing is not prevented from being an object of personal property rights merely because it is neither a thing in possession nor a thing in action.

That recognition is important, but it does not determine who owns a particular asset, which law applies, whether a claim is enforceable or whether any practical recovery route exists. Those questions depend on the asset, transaction history, evidence, parties, jurisdiction and available remedies. They require assessment by appropriately qualified independent specialists.

Technical control and legal ownership

A private key ordinarily provides technical control over a digital asset. Technical control may be highly relevant evidence, but it is not necessarily conclusive of legal ownership in every circumstance. Equally, loss of access to a key does not by itself resolve the question of title.

Claims involving theft, fraud, mistaken transfer, lost access, insolvency, custody or competing interests can raise materially different issues. Transfers to later recipients may also involve tracing questions and legal defences. No single principle or slogan determines the outcome.

Potential pathways

Depending on the facts and applicable law, independent specialists may consider legal, forensic or technical pathways. These can involve identifying relevant parties, preserving evidence, tracing transactions, seeking information or protective orders, establishing ownership or pursuing other remedies. The availability, cost and prospects of any pathway are case-specific.

Neither legal recognition of an asset as property nor proof of an earlier interest guarantees restoration, access or financial recovery. Courts, counterparties and technical systems may impose practical limits, and remedies can differ substantially between jurisdictions.

Our name: “To Honesty”

Our name reflects the standards we aim to bring to every introduction: integrity, accountability, candour and respect for client confidentiality. We believe prospective clients should receive a clear explanation of our limited role, avoid unrealistic promises and remain free to evaluate any independent specialist and proposed engagement for themselves.

Our commitment

We provide a controlled first point of contact and a carefully managed introduction process. We minimise the information requested at the outset and never ask for private keys, seed phrases, passwords, PINs, passphrases, recovery codes or direct access to a wallet, account, device or asset.

We do not determine legal ownership, evaluate evidence, predict the outcome of proceedings or promise that assets can be recovered. Where an enquiry falls within our referral criteria, our purpose is to help the prospective client reach an independent specialist capable of providing an informed assessment.
