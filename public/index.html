Minimal Access Protocol

Only the minimum contact and evidentiary-status information is collected. It is encrypted in your browser before transmission.

A restrained first step

Discretion before detail.

The first contact should establish whether an introduction may be appropriate—not collect the facts of your loss. We do not request wallet addresses, transaction histories, asset values or recovery credentials at intake.

01

Independent introduction

We assess potential suitability and, with your explicit consent, may introduce you to independent specialist partners.

02

Ownership-led approach

Private keys provide technical control. Legal ownership is established through evidence and applicable law.

03

Strict boundaries

Tohonesty is not a law firm, recovery firm, financial adviser or asset custodian, and does not execute recovery operations.
